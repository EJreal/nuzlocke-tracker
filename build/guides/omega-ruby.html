Omega Ruby Encounters

A fun aspect of Nuzlocke runs is the variety of encounters forcing you to try out new Pokémon you've never used before. That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Omega Ruby so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Omega Ruby has 80 different encounter locations with 259 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!

Omega Ruby Boss Battles

In order to complete a Omega Ruby Nuzlocke and become the Champion, you will need to win 28 Boss battles throughout the Hoenn region - ranging from bickering Rival & Evil team fights, to Gym Leaders & ultimately the Elite Four.

These can be a challenge, especially when your dear nicknamed nuzlocke mons are at risk. So below we've listed detailed overviews of all these fights! Giving you all the information you'll need to face everything from Rival's Mudkip to Drake's Salamence.

Select your starter type

Like all Pokémon games, you will have to select a starter. The type will update some of the boss teams to match. For example, if your starter was a grass type Rival might have a fire type to take advantage!
