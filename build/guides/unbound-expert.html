That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Unbound Expert so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Unbound Expert has 61 different encounter locations with 467 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
