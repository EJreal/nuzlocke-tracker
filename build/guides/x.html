In order to complete a X Nuzlocke and become the Champion, you will need to win 39 Boss battles throughout the Kalos region - ranging from bickering Rival & Evil team fights, to Gym Leaders & ultimately the Elite Four.

These can be a challenge, especially when your dear nicknamed nuzlocke mons are at risk. So below we've listed detailed overviews of all these fights! Giving you all the information you'll need to face everything from Shauna's Chespin to Drasna's Noivern.

Select your starter type

Like all Pokémon games, you will have to select a starter. The type will update some of the boss teams to match. For example, if your starter was a grass type Shauna might have a fire type to take advantage!
