Brilliant Diamond Encounters

A fun aspect of Nuzlocke runs is the variety of encounters forcing you to try out new Pokémon you've never used before. That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Brilliant Diamond so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Brilliant Diamond has 73 different encounter locations with 232 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
