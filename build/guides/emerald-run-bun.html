That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Emerald Run & Bun so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Emerald Run & Bun has 71 different encounter locations with 462 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
