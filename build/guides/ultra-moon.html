That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Ultra Moon so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Ultra Moon has 72 different encounter locations with 312 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
