That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Storm Silver so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Storm Silver has 96 different encounter locations with 377 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
