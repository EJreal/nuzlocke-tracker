That is why it's important to know which Pokémon will be available during a run. We've compiled a list of every encounter for Pokémon Inclement Emerald so you can quickly see who you might want on your team!

Pokémon Inclement Emerald has 78 different encounter locations with 476 different Pokémon available as wild encounters. Hover over a Pokémon to see where to encounter it!
